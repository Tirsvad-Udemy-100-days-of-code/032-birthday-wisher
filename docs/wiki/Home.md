# Birthday Wisher

🌐 **English** · [Dansk](Home-da) · [Bahasa Melayu](Home-ms)

Sends a random birthday letter by e-mail to everyone in `birthdays.csv` whose birthday is today. It is the "normal" level of day 32 of Udemy's *100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp*.
